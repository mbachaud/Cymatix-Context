# W2.1 — COVER-edge walk (append-below-head + eps-band) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A default-inert query-time walk over the ingest-built `gene_relations`
COVER graph that (a) appends walk-rescued documents into the tail of the final
top-k and (b) adds walk mass as an eps-band-confined rerank class — then one
paired ladder arm on the v09x bed.

**Architecture:** One pure walk module (`retrieval/cover_walk.py`, modeled on
the SR walker) computes a confidence-weighted, degree-capped, γ-damped mass
spread from the fused head over `gene_relations relation=5` (bidirectional
when the new reverse index exists, forward-only otherwise). `query_docs` uses
the mass twice on the RRF path only: pre-`combine_rerank` as a
`"cover_walk"` rerank class (only when the effective combinator is
`eps_band` — never a free additive), and post-#341-seam as an
append-below-head that fills the last `append_slots` positions of the final
order with the highest-mass documents not already in the kept head
(displaced head docs slide below, they do not vanish). Everything is
observable via `last_cover_walk_diag` and a `cover_walk` per-signal timing.

**Tech Stack:** stdlib sqlite3, existing Fuser/rerank machinery. No new deps.

**Spec:** `docs/research/2026-08-28-wave2-semantic-ranking-graph-research.md`
(lane 1 conditions + pre-flight F1) and its skeptic conditions: (i) COVER
substrate, (ii) degree-cap/hub guard, (iii) append-below-head or
eps-band-confined only, (iv) F1 passed (receipt
`w2_cover_reachability_preflight_2026-08-28.json`).

## Global Constraints

- Default-inert: `cover_walk_enabled = false`; with the knob off, retrieval
  is byte-identical (no walk call, no new SQL).
- RRF path only (`fusion_mode == "rrf"`); the additive legacy path is
  untouched.
- Band additive applies ONLY when the effective per-query combinator is
  `eps_band` (the shipped all-classes default); under `additive`/`off`/
  `fused_tier` the walk contributes append-only.
- Read-only stores stay read-only (walk is SELECT-only).
- No bed mutation during the arm window; the reverse-index migration runs
  BEFORE the arm and its new `bed_bytes` is recorded in the arm receipt
  (phase sequencing rules 5/6).
- Cold-bed house rule: the arm launches ≥1 h after any manager-mediated bed
  access (including the single effective-lever probe).
- Paired basis: same 470-needle sequence as
  `ladder_v09x_w1c_k20_eps_all_full_2026-08-28.json` (the pairing reference).

## Registered predictions (write BEFORE the arm runs)

1. Most semantic lift comes from 1-hop rescues (F1: 16 static + 18 stalled
   gold at 1 hop of the top-50 head, median frontier ~960). Target: semantic
   delivered 0.312 → ≥0.36; stretch 0.44.
2. 2-hop rescues are rare (median 2-hop frontier ~47k — mass dilution).
3. Displacement cost: needles with gold at final ranks 10–12 can lose when
   3 rescues displace those slots; watch cat-(b) ids (erb_176, 181, 187,
   237, 272) and non-semantic paired losses.
4. Decision rule: graduate-candidate iff paired semantic delivered net ≥ +5
   AND overall delivered net ≥ 0 AND no question-type delivered regression
   > 0.05. Kill iff overall delivered net < −5 or semantic net ≤ 0.

### Task 2: Walk module

**Files:**
- Create: `cymatix_context/retrieval/cover_walk.py`
- Test: `tests/test_cover_walk.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `cover_walk_mass(genome, seed_ids, *, hops=2, gamma=0.5,
  degree_cap=1000, frontier_cap=2000) -> tuple[dict[str, float], dict]` —
  `{gene_id: mass}` for every reached NON-seed node, plus a diag dict
  `{"mode": "bidirectional"|"forward_only"|"absent", "frontier": [per-hop
  new-node counts], "seeds_in_graph": int}`. Missing `gene_relations` table
  → `({}, {"mode": "absent", ...})`. Never raises out.

Semantics: uniform seed mass `1/len(seeds)`; per hop, a node with total
degree > `degree_cap` is not expanded (hub guard — it can still RECEIVE
mass); a node's outgoing share is `gamma * mass * conf_i / sum(conf)` over
its (capped) edge set (mass-conserving, confidence-weighted); frontier
truncated to top-`frontier_cap` by mass per hop (SR precedent); adjacency
fetched in bulk `IN (...)` batches of ≤500 ids per direction; the reverse
direction is used only when `idx_gene_relations_rel_b` exists (one
`PRAGMA index_list` probe per call).

## RESULTS — w2_cover_walk arm (2026-08-28, 470 needles, cold bed, receipt `ladder_v09x_w2_cover_walk_2026-08-28.json`)

**VERDICT: KILLED, per the pre-registered decision rule** (semantic
delivered net = 0 ≤ 0). Paired vs `ladder_v09x_w1c_k20_eps_all_full`
(same needle sequence; arm ran 14:24–17:18, launched 1 h after the last
manager-mediated access):

| basis | arm | ref | paired |
|---|---|---|---|
| map r@12 | 0.6809 | 0.6809 | +0/−0 (by construction — the walk never touches the score map) |
| final fr@12 | **0.6553** | 0.6830 | **+1/−14** |
| delivered | 0.6298 | 0.6298 | **+0/−0** (identical on every needle, every type) |

**Mechanism attribution (complete):**

1. **The append lane never reached delivery.** Assembly sorts candidates
   by `last_query_scores`; rescues carry no map score, sort last, and are
   cut/evicted before expression — so the walk changed the published
   final order but not one delivered set in 470. A delivery-honoring
   append would need a score-map seat or an assembly-order bypass. (Not
   worth building — see 3.)
2. **Displacement is real and predicted.** All 14 final-basis losses are
   needles whose gold sat at ref final rank 10–12 and was pushed to
   exactly 13–15 by the 3 append slots (`gold_displaced=True` in every
   per-needle diag; 39 needles had gold displaced overall). The cat-(b)
   ids (erb_181/187/237/272) are among them — prediction 3 confirmed.
3. **The core bet failed: COVER mass does not discriminate paraphrastic
   gold.** 470/470 queries appended 3 rescues (1,410 slots); gold appeared
   in 16 — and 14/16 were multi-gold needles already hit through a
   different gold gene (map ranks 1–9, n_gold 2–18). Genuine novel
   rescue: **1** (erb_276, semantic, stalled at map rank 22). Top-3 mass
   precision ≈ 1%: the walk's mass concentrates on shared-entity hub
   neighbors — the corpus-ubiquitous-entity confound the crater anatomy
   predicted, now measured. The eps-band class fired (band_boosted > 0
   throughout) and produced zero measurable order improvement.
4. The 72% 2-hop reachability from pre-flight F1 was real but is
   membership, not discrimination — reach ≠ rescue, exactly the caveat
   the pre-flight receipt registered.

**Disposition:** `cover_walk_enabled` stays default-false (fully inert;
suite-pinned). The infrastructure ships with this receipt as the
refutation record: the wave-2 skeptic's open bet ("no published system
demonstrates paraphrase-gold lift via co-occurrence walks") resolves
NEGATIVE on this corpus at these knobs. W2.3 (banded combiner over walk
features) dies with it. W2.2 (entity-overlap in-band discrimination, cat
(c) only) is untouched by this result and still gated on the F2 audit.
The crater's remaining live lanes are W2.2-narrow and the second-corpus
paraphrase split (EnronQA rephrased) for external confirmation.
